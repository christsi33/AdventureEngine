Welcome to Project: Surface!
You have awakened in an abandoned underground facility. Your ultimate goal is to navigate
through the dark rooms,avoid deadly hazards, and unlock the Main Gate to escape to the surface.

--- HOW TO PLAY ---
The game is purely text-based. You interact with the world by typing commands into the console.
The game features a smart parser that ignores words like "the", "to", "at", so you can type naturally!

--- BASIC COMMANDS ---
Here are the essential commands you need to survive:

1. MOVEMENT
   - Command: go <direction>
   - Example: "go north", "go west"
   - Synonyms: move, walk, run, head

2. OBSERVATION
   - Command: look / look at <target>
   - Description: Type "look" to scan the whole room, or "look at <item>" to examine a specific point of interest.
   - Example: "look at electrical panel"
   - Synonyms: inspect, examine, search, x

3. INTERACTION & ITEMS
   - Command: take <item>
   - Example: "take flashlight", "take blue keycard"
   - Synonyms: grab, collect, pick up

   - Command: open <target> with <item>
   - Description: Used to unlock doors or mechanisms using items from your inventory.
   - Example: "open gate with red key"
   - Synonyms: unlock, use

4. INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
   - Command: inventory
   - Description: Shows all the items you are currently carrying.
   - Synonyms: inv, bag, items, i

5. GAME STATE
   - Command: save (Saves your current progress)
   - Command: load (Restores your last saved game)
   - Command: quit (Exits the game)

--- SURVIVAL TIPS ---
* Always "look" around when you enter a new room to discover hidden items and exits.
* Beware of Traps! Interacting with certain objects without the proper equipment (like gloves or a gas mask) will result in instant death.
* Read the room descriptions carefully; they often contain hints about what items are required to progress.

Good luck. The surface awaits.
